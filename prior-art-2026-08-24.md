# Prior art review, 2026-08-24

Why this exists: item R5 was about to be written around the claim that this
repository's evidence linked claim machinery is a novel contribution. Before
writing that down, the claim was checked. **It does not survive.** The pattern is
a decade old, has independent 2026 implementations in speech, and one narrow
version of the claim survives.

This document is the record so nobody researches it again. It is a literature
review, not a finding about speech.

## The question

Is "a language model writes prose over a structured measurement record, then a
checker mechanically verifies the model's numeric claims against that record"
novel as of August 2026?

## The answer

No. It is well established, and in four separate literatures.

### The direct ancestor is not a language model system at all

**statcheck**, Nuijten et al., 2015 onward. It extracts statistics from
psychology papers and recomputes the p value from the test statistic and degrees
of freedom, flagging inconsistencies and decision flipping inconsistencies.
Reported classification accuracy 96.2 to 99.9 percent. It is GPL, and it is
embedded in peer review at *Psychological Science* and *JESP*.

- Prevalence study: https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC5101263/
- Tool paper: https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1002/jrsm.1408
- https://statcheck.io/

This is the claim ledger's idea, from a decade ago, without the model. Cite it
as an ancestor rather than pretend it is not there.

### The language model version is canonical

- **Wiseman et al. 2017**, *Challenges in Data-to-Document Generation*, EMNLP.
  Introduced RotoWire, generating basketball summaries from box scores, and the
  extractive evaluation that pulls relations back out of the generated text and
  compares them against the source records.
  https://aclanthology.org/D17-1239/
- **PARENT**, Dhingra et al., ACL 2019. Scores generated text against the source
  table rather than only against a reference. https://aclanthology.org/P19-1483/
- Numeric infidelity in data to text has been a named failure mode since at
  least the 2022 hallucination survey. https://arxiv.org/pdf/2202.03629

### This project's specific design already exists as a general protocol

**Proof-Carrying Numbers**, Solatorio, World Bank, 8 September 2025,
CC BY-SA 4.0. https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.06902

A presentation layer protocol in which numbers are emitted as claim bound tokens
and a verifier checks each numeric span against a declared policy of exact,
rounded or tolerance, fail closed, with unverified numbers left unmarked. That
is `verify_claim_ledger` and its tolerance, specified generically and domain
agnostically a year before this repository built it. It has never been applied to
speech.

### And it exists in speech, twice, from June 2026

- **SpeakerCard-1M**, BUT Speech@FIT, arXiv 2606.03283, 28 June 2026.
  https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.03283
  States an explicit **tool first, language model last** principle. Ten acoustic
  probes populate a structured schema; the model verbalises those fields and
  **never sees the raw audio**; a quality control pipeline then checks the
  generated card against its structured premises by natural language inference
  entailment. That is claim verification of model prose against a measurement
  record, in speech. Annotations and splits released, audio not.
- **Prior over Evidence**, Wang and Sun, arXiv 2606.15325, 13 June 2026.
  https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.15325
  1,800 L2-ARCTIC utterances, three multimodal models, five evidence conditions.
  **39.6 percent of judged cells contained internally coherent reasoning
  supporting a wrong rating, against 15.8 percent coherent and correct.** Phoneme
  level feedback collapsed onto a fixed inventory regardless of first language,
  and hiding gender labels reversed the scoring bias. Their conclusion is this
  project's thesis in their own words: current general purpose models are more
  reliable as verbalisers of externally computed evidence than as standalone
  diagnostic engines.

Two independent groups posted this architecture within two weeks of each other.
That is a converging front, not an empty field.

### Open implementations of the general pattern are commodity

OpenFactCheck (https://openfactcheck.com/), MedVAL with open weights and a
PhysioNet benchmark (https://github.com/StanfordMIMI/MedVAL), RadFact under MIT
(https://github.com/microsoft/RadFact), GREEN
(https://github.com/Stanford-AIMI/GREEN), plus RAGAS, DeepEval and guardrail
libraries. Radiology alone has ten years of report factuality metrics: CheXpert
labeler, CheXbert, RadGraph and RadGraph F1, RadCliQ, RadFact, GREEN,
FineRadScore, and a 2025 to 2026 wave.

## Where it is actually thin

Two places, both narrower than "the most publishable thing here".

1. **Deterministic rather than model judged verification of numerals against a
   machine produced record, as a first class artifact.** The closest statement of
   this as a thesis is *Deterministic Integrity Gates for LLM-Assisted Clinical
   Manuscript Preparation*, arXiv 2606.09500, 8 June 2026, MIT licensed:
   deterministic gates caught 27 of 27 seeded defects where a generic language
   model reviewer caught 11. Two months old and in another domain. Supporting
   argument for choosing determinism: arXiv 2604.11133, April 2026, documents 10
   to 30 percent model error rates on simple clinical numerical comparisons.
2. **Speech and voice.** No system computes acoustic measures, has a model write
   prose over them, and mechanically verifies the prose against the measures.
   SpeakerCard does the architecture for speaker traits, with no uncertainty and
   no abstention.

## The one claim that survives, and it is an integration claim

> No open, reusable harness combines provenance on inputs, per measurement
> uncertainty, explicit abstention, and verification of generated claims, for
> speech measurement.

Checked against the field: openSMILE (source available, not open licensed),
Parselmouth, DisVoice, Surfboard, speechmetrics, VERSA, SpeechBrain, ESPnet3,
OpenDBM, TELL 2.0. Every one returns a number and stops. None attaches per
measurement uncertainty, none emits an evidence link back to a span of the source
audio, none has an abstention path, and none verifies downstream prose. The
nearest neighbours are ProvenAI, which does provenance for retrieval augmented
text rather than speech, and MerkleSpeech, which does provenance of the audio
bytes rather than of measurements derived from them.

**This is a claim about integration and licence, not about invention.** Write it
that way.

## Two further findings worth keeping

**Abstention in speaking assessment is solved commercially and undocumented
publicly.** ETS holds patents on non scorable response filters, US 8,990,082 and
US 9,704,413, covering audio quality, insufficient speech, off topic, wrong
language and plagiarised content, each independently enable able. It is a genuine
abstention architecture with per criterion evidence, and it is proprietary. The
academic literature on model based speech feedback does not abstain at all: the
SLaTE 2025 zero shot paper's specific failure was over predicting scores on low
quality speech, which is exactly what abstention would have caught. Duolingo's
2025 fairness study excluded poor audio samples by hand, which is manual
abstention nobody has automated.

**Deterministic core with an optional model layer is a normal architecture, not
an odd one.** It is actively advocated in 2026:

- FORTHought, arXiv 2605.13245, May 2026: "the model orchestrates deterministic
  tools rather than generating analytical code".
- A seven channel EEG agent, arXiv 2606.26519, June 2026, open source, uses
  boundary aware context grounding to keep the model inside what the measurements
  support.
- An ECG and PCG coupling system, *Algorithms* 19(2):117, February 2026, uses the
  model as "a strictly constrained semantic interface" performing no autonomous
  interpretation.
- A bearing fault diagnosis system, arXiv 2607.22797, July 2026, constrains the
  model "to translate, but never generate, diagnostic content", reporting
  unsupported claim rate falling from 10 to 12 percent down to 2 percent.

## The argument that does not come from the literature

Neither the recommendation nor the papers say this, and it is the sharpest point
against making the verifier the headline.

**Verification is only as interesting as the model's freedom to be wrong.**

Today the model invents five scores and the verifier checks the numbers it
quotes: the last acceptance run found 6 numeric claims, verified 6, and caught
nothing. Remove the scores and constrain the model to describe measurements, and
its numeric claims become restatements of `master.json`. The verifier then checks
that a copy operation copied correctly. **Six of six verified is not evidence of a
strong verifier; it is evidence the task was easy.** In production the verifier
has never caught anything. The only demonstrated catch is a synthetic unit test.

Worse, the failure that matters is unsupported *interpretation*, not arithmetic.
That is the 39.6 percent Prior over Evidence measured, and none of it is numeric.
A numeric verifier cannot catch it.

So the reframe that verification.md makes the model the subject of measurement
rather than the person is right in spirit and **currently unearned**. It is an
architecture, not a measurement. Earning it needs a reported rate across several
models over the same records, which is deferred in `improvement-plan.md` section
5a.

# Motor speech and voice governance review package

Version: 1.0.0 draft

Status: adults-first scope recorded; external governance has not started;
checkpoint 23B is in progress and every research lane remains unselected

Updated: 2026-08-14

This package is the material an independent professional, lived-experience
partner, research institution, ethics body, privacy and security reviewer,
statistician and Australian regulatory specialist would need to review before
any task, participant work or implementation could be approved. It is not an
ethics application, legal opinion, clinical protocol or regulatory decision.

The machine-checkable state is
`governance-contract-v1.0.0.json`. Its validator deliberately accepts only the
truth today: Adam approved checkpoint 23B and an adults-first research scope,
but no external authority, task, method, participant source, data store or
downstream checkpoint has been approved.

Blank human decision records are in `governance-record-templates.md`. They make
future reviewers state competence, conflicts, evidence, limits and decisions;
they are not completed reviews or substitutes for an institution's forms.
`final_decision.py` defines the separate machine rules for a future signed
closure, including responsible opaque assignments and a closed, chronological
hash dependency graph from the parent contract through candidate records,
reviews and lane decisions to the owner-issued overall decision. The validator
cannot judge private professional substance; the accountable institution and
release group must verify the private bytes, signatures and meaning. No final
decision artifact exists.

### Route two: an independent registered HREC and separate sponsor governance

## Conflict and recusal rules

Every person completes a conflict record before receiving non-public project
material. It covers employment, ownership, equity, patents, royalties,
consulting, referral fees, grants, publications, supervision, clinical
dependencies and reputational interests.

- No vendor employee, founder or equity holder votes on candidate eligibility,
  reference truth or release.
- No payment depends on a selection, threshold, favourable finding or release.
- A conflicted member may provide factual evidence, then leaves deliberation and
  voting for the affected question.
- A person cannot design the algorithm, create its reference truth, open held-out
  evidence and recommend release.
- A treating clinician does not recruit or consent their dependent client when
  an independent route is feasible.
- Adam controls product scope and spending but cannot override clinical,
  participant, institutional, ethics, privacy, legal, statistical or regulatory
  blocks.
- All abstentions, recusals, minority views and unresolved disagreements are
  retained in the decision artifact.

6. access barriers, confounders, burden, safety and foreseeable harms;
7. capture, device, room and recording requirements;
8. computational definition and independent verification route;
9. prospective estimand, repetitions, raters or listeners, missingness and

10. representation and unsupported-group rules;
11. institution, ethics, privacy, consent, rights, vendor and regulatory

12. conditions that force lane-specific `no_selection`.

The current unordered questions are:

## Task, access, burden and stop review

Governance may approve a review template but cannot yet fill task values. Each
future task dossier must freeze:

- prompt and version;
- instruction wording, presentation mode, demonstration and comprehension
  check;
- comfortable or maximum effort;
- practice, feedback, repetitions, order, rest, duration and retry rules;
- reading, hearing, memory, language, motor, respiratory and device demands;
- accessible information, AAC, interpreter, support-person and non-reading
  routes;
- whether an accommodation creates a genuinely different task;
- capture device, application, codec, sample rate, distance, room and operator;
- complete, partial, declined, stopped, misunderstood, access-incompatible,
  protocol-deviated and recording-invalid states;
- participant-reported effort, discomfort, misunderstanding and desire to stop;
- immediate stopping for a participant signal, pain, unusual breathlessness,
  dizziness, cough, distress, marked fatigue, researcher concern or invalid
  equipment;
- static Australian Stroke Foundation FAST wording for sudden new slurred
  speech, without automatic listening, triage or reassurance.

An incomplete or inaccessible attempt becomes `could_not_assess`. It never
becomes zero, normal, poor performance or a reason to exclude the participant
after seeing a result.

## Reference-manual requirements

### Task fidelity

The record keeps task version, delivery, comprehension evidence, deviations,
participant confirmation, capture provenance, attempt state and an independent
reviewer blinded to algorithm output. Acoustic conformity cannot prove that the
person understood the task.

### Rapid syllable timing and accuracy, if retained

The manual must separately define:

- syllable, token, cycle and sequence;
- consonant onset, vowel onset, offset and cycle landmarks;
- first and last partial cycles;
- substitution, omission, addition, distortion, wrong order, self-correction,
  false start, breath, cough, laugh, interruption and background speech;
- timing annotations versus observable accuracy annotations;
- ambiguity, confidence and `unresolvable` states;
- sample-index time base, annotation tool, version, display and precision;
- two independent trained annotations, seeded intra-rater repeats, retained
  disagreement and prospective adjudication;
- full reannotation rules after a material manual change.

The task cannot imply tongue or jaw strength, exact articulator movement,
disease, ordinary communication, severity or diagnosis.

### Connected speech, if retained

Reading, picture description, monologue and conversation are different tasks.
The manual separately defines sample start and end, intended material,
adjudicated actual production, word and syllable counting, total versus speaking
time, silent and filled pauses, breaths, disfluencies, repetition, repairs,
false starts, reading errors, overlap, examiner speech and invalid material. A
pause threshold is part of the construct and cannot be borrowed casually.

### Voice reference, if retained

No voice protocol is selected. A future perceptual protocol needs:

- one exact descriptive construct, never pathology, cause, health or normality;
- several qualified independent adult voice raters;
- preserved individual ratings and disagreement;
- frozen stimuli, instructions, order, replay, equipment, listening environment,
  playback level, form version and licence;
- task-specific results rather than pooling vowels, sentences and connected
  speech;
- blinding to algorithm, participant, clinical and development information;
- rater eligibility, conflicts, language or variety familiarity, hearing,
  workload, fatigue, calibration and drift controls;
- intra-rater and absolute inter-rater agreement, confidence intervals and
  measurement error rather than correlation alone;
- explicit protection against an ideal accent, gender presentation, pitch,
  loudness, resonance or voice.

The revised CAPE-V is only a protocol family for later review. It is not
selected. Commercial incorporation has an ASHA permission route that must not
be contacted or assumed without approval.

### Participant report, if relevant

The participant is authoritative for their own goals, experience, impact,
identity and participation. Assistance may be provided, but the record preserves
whose experience is reported. A later instrument review needs target-population
content validity, accessibility, comprehension, reliability, measurement error,
responsiveness, cross-cultural validity, recall period, burden, missingness,
support rules, licence and data rights. No PROM is selected.

### Controlled intelligibility, if relevant

The record keeps intended material, independently adjudicated actual production
and each blinded unfamiliar listener's orthographic transcription separately.
Task fidelity, intelligibility, naturalness, comprehension, listening effort and
participation are not pooled. Listener language, hearing, familiarity,
adaptation, exposure, workload and speaker or prompt overlap are frozen. A
clinician estimate or ASR confidence cannot substitute.

### Clinical reference, if relevant

Clinical evidence requires an independent comprehensive professional assessment
and any medical, laryngeal, hearing, cognitive or language evidence required by
the exact claim. Diagnosis can define a study population but is not numeric
ground truth for an acoustic primitive. Audio cannot establish cause.

## Adults-first population and representation

The eligibility boundary is 18 and over. Exact age need not enter product
identity. Research age bands or dates may be collected only if the statistician,
professional group, privacy reviewer and ethics pathway find them necessary and
the participant separately consents.

The study frame must decide whether it concerns a general adult population,
people with self-identified concerns, people receiving care, people with an
independently established condition, or another group. These populations cannot
be pooled to make one look representative.

Disability, communication difference, accent, multilingual use, inability to
read, severe speech difference or algorithm failure are not automatic
exclusions. The protocol must cover the Australian English varieties,
Aboriginal Englishes, multilingual adults, gender-diverse voices, disability,
professional voice use, regional and remote access, and devices that its claim
actually includes. Unsupported groups receive `unsupported`, not a default
result.

If Aboriginal or Torres Strait Islander people or data are included or affected,
community-led governance under the NHMRC Indigenous ethical guidelines must
precede recruitment. A generic consumer seat is insufficient.

## Prospective sampling and split framework

No numeric sample size is justified before a construct, primary estimand and
pilot variance exist. “Thirty” or “one hundred” is not a scientific plan.

The independent statistician must prospectively receive:

- one primary estimand per retained question;
- expected between-person and within-person variance;
- acceptable precision for bias, limits of agreement, reliability, invalidity
  and abstention;
- number of repeated performances, raters and listeners;
- speaker-by-listener variation if intelligibility is retained;
- expected withdrawal, partial tasks, unusable recordings and missing reference;
- household, site, clinician, device, prompt and repeated-session cluster effects;
- minimum evidence for every claimed subgroup;
- supported task, device, environment, language and variety strata;
- multiplicity and number of candidate questions;
- recruitment feasibility, burden and cost.

Participant is the minimum split unit. Every session and clip from one adult
stays together. Households, sites, clinicians, prompts or device clusters also
stay together where overlap could leak information. Anything seen during 23C is
permanently pilot or development evidence.

A future independent custodian runs a frozen allocation before 23D outcomes or
labels are inspected, publishes aggregate split proof and keeps held-out
identities inaccessible. Held-out evidence is opened once only after method,
manual, task, code and analysis freeze. Exact proportions remain unresolved.

## Privacy, consent and security worksheet

### Entity and applicable law

Qualified review must name:

- the legal sponsor and responsible institution;
- every legal entity or person that collects, holds, uses, discloses, accesses
  or exercises effective control over information, plus its contractual service
  role, jurisdiction, vendors, countries and subprocessors;
- whether each relevant entity is an APP entity and whether private
  health-service provider coverage applies despite the small-business
  threshold;
- applicable state or territory health-records and surveillance or
  listening-device laws, including participant and speaker location, recorder
  and operator location, device and server location, local, phone or VoIP
  capture, interception in transit, human listening and transcription, and
  later use, disclosure, communication or publication;
- any site, hospital, university, funder, insurer or contract requirements;
- the privacy contact and complaint route.

Queensland generally permits a party to record their own private conversation,
but restricts later communication or publication. That is not permission to
collect research audio without explicit consent. Legal review must map every
location and operation above, including Commonwealth interception questions;
state recording rules materially differ. No public guide substitutes for
qualified advice on the final design.

The design first prevents incidental speakers with a private room, headset and
visible notice. If another voice is captured, the recording is immediately
quarantined and receives no transcription, annotation, model use, sharing or
analysis. It is minimised, redacted and destroyed under the approved protocol,
with only a content-free event log. Release requires the person's consent plus
the applicable legal, institutional and ethics authority. Those controls remain
requirements, not approved operations.

### Data flow

The final PIA must draw collection, temporary capture, upload, storage, human
review, analysis, export, backup, deletion, breach and destruction flows. It
names countries, regions, subprocessors, encryption, key ownership, access
roles, logging, administrator access, model training, secondary use, incident
response and verified deletion.

The PIA also needs an APP 5 collection notice and APP 6 use-and-disclosure map.
The accessible notice identifies the collecting entity, circumstances and
purposes, consequences if core information is not provided, usual recipients,
access, correction and complaint routes, and likely overseas countries where
practicable. A use or disclosure outside the primary purpose needs its own
lawful and governed basis; consent is not used as a blanket permission.

No overseas processing or secondary model training is allowed by default. APP
8 accountability and every vendor's actual contract need review before any
transfer. A public privacy policy is not a data-processing agreement.

### Separate consent decisions

The future record separates mandatory core study elements from genuinely
optional uses. Core elements are:

- research participation;
- audio recording;
- human review where it is part of the approved reference process.

An adult may freely decline any core element, but declining audio means they do
not join that audio study; it is not recorded as failure or poor performance.
The following optional choices default to decline and do not affect normal
product access:

- raw audio retention;
- derived data retention;
- future secondary research, if proposed;
- fairness metadata, if scientifically necessary;
- external transfer, if proposed;
- future recontact, if proposed.

Every choice uses a versioned accessible notice. Consent cannot be inferred from
speech, continued use or another consent. Support, AAC or a representative must
not erase who made the decision or whose experience is reported.

Withdrawal always stops the person's further participation. The approved notice
must state separately for raw audio, identifiers, annotations, derived data,
analysed data, backups and published aggregates whether and until when future
use stops, deletion is possible, or retention is required. The institution,
study type and applicable law determine retention; this draft promises no
period. Qualified review must reconcile any research retention duty with APP 11
destruction or de-identification requirements and explain immutable audit,
backup expiry and published-result limits before consent.

  without diagnosis, cause, severity, health, normality or identity conformity;
- task fidelity cannot be established independently of the measured output;
- accessible task versions cannot be defined without pooling incompatible tasks;
- a reference manual cannot define landmarks, ambiguity and disagreement;
- intended prompt, actual production, timing, perception, intelligibility,
  participant report and clinical evidence cannot remain separate;
- independent reference truth would be controlled by the developer or vendor;
- institution, ethics, consent, lawful source, commercial rights, privacy,
  security, retention, withdrawal or deletion remains unresolved;
- a statistician cannot design feasible precision, repetitions, subgroup
  evidence and missingness handling;
- invalidity or abstention would selectively erase adults the claim includes;
- device, room, task, language or variety dependence cannot be examined;
- participant-exclusive splits, overlap control or held-out custody are unsafe;
- safety, emergency, complaints or adverse-event routes are not accepted;
- regulatory classification or trial obligations are unresolved;
- any accountable role does not sign within its domain.

Voice also records `no_selection` if it has no justified value beyond item 20,
requires an ideal voice or composite index, lacks a lawful perceptual or PROM
protocol, or implies laryngeal truth without an independent medical route.

A final 23B `no_selection` lists exact reasons and evidence needed to reopen,
sets checkpoints 23C through 23E to `not_applicable`, and proves no participant,
private participant or held-out data was accessed. It separately records whether
private professional or governance evidence was reviewed under approved
controls; a signed external rejection normally requires that access.
